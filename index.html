Why Copenhagen?

Copenhagen, the chapmen's haven

Copenhagen, derives its name from Old Norse and Old English roots meaning "merchants' harbour" or "buyer's haven" (Køpmannæhafn), sharing an etymological root with the word chapman (an itinerant dealer or merchant). Multiple finds indicate that Copenhagen originated in the 11th century. The first habitations were probably centred on Gammel Strand (literally 'old shore') in the 11th century or even earlier.

Nowadays, Copenhagen has become the centre of a larger metropolitan area spanning Denmark and Sweden. The Øresund Bridge has brought about considerable changes in public transport and has led to the extensive redevelopment of Amager, Denmark's most densely populated island. The city's service and trade sectors developed and a number of banking and financial institutions were established.

My favorite cafés in Copenhagen

Hart Bageri

Hart has pushed the boundaries of what Danish patries looks like and has gained almost an iconic status after its wild popularity both in Denmark and abroad.

Address:

Gl. Kongevej 109

What I like about it

In a city that loves baked goods as much as Copenhagen, Hart still stands out from the crowd with its sky-high level of quality.

BUKA

BUKA is a contemporary eatery located in the heart of Copenhagen, known for its vibrant atmosphere and innovative dishes.

Address:

Store Kongensgade 18

What I like about it

BUKA creates a unique dining experience that attracts both locals and visitors. The menu features a variety of seasonal dishes, showcasing fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

Next Door Café

Inside the café, there’s a very homey atmosphere. Clearly a local spot frequented by a lot of regulars, Next Door Café welcome everyone in need of good coffee in relaxed surroundings.

Address:

Larsbjørnsstræde 23

What I like about it

Under the glass plates on the tables, you’ll find greetings, notes, drawings and pictures tucked in there by gratified guests. And to top it all off, Skyler bakes everything himself after his own recipes (except the lemon squares – that’s mom’s recipe).
